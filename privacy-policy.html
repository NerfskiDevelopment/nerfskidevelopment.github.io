Ghoul Valley Privacy Policy

Effective date November 13, 2026

This policy applies to the Ghoul Valley website, game client, and game servers. Nerfski Development operates Ghoul Valley and is responsible for the personal information described here. For privacy questions or requests, email nerfskidevelopment@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information What it includes and why we use it
Account and sign-in Your username, email address, password hash, and email verification records. We use these to create and secure your account, verify your email, and help you regain access.
Connection and security We store your IP address, platform, and client version with your account. We use this information to troubleshoot, protect accounts, and investigate abuse.
Gameplay Your character profile, progress, stats, area, items, pets, Shards, PayVault purchases, and friends list. We use these records to run the game, save progress, and support your account.
Social features Public chat, private messages, and player activity that the game displays to others. We process public chat and private messages for delivery. We do not retain chat or private message contents or record IP addresses in message logs. We may use information players submit in reports to investigate misconduct.
Payments A checkout or payment reference, purchase amount and currency, purchase status, and the Shards credited to your account. We use these to deliver purchases, resolve errors, and handle refunds, disputes, and accounting.
Support Information you include when you contact us, such as your email, username, message, and attachments. We use it to respond and resolve your request.

We use account and gameplay data to provide the game you request. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our agreement to provide the service, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, and legal obligations for matters such as financial records. If we rely on consent for an optional activity, you can withdraw it.

What other players can see

Other players may see your username, appearance, level, visible pets, and presence or in-game area through gameplay and social features. Public chat can be read by players who receive it. Private messages are delivered to the selected player, who can copy or share what you send. Please keep personal and payment details out of chat.

Who receives information

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) provides hosting. Email providers help deliver verification emails.
  • Stripe processes purchases through Stripe Checkout. Payment details are entered on Stripe's checkout page. Ghoul Valley receives order and payment confirmation information for fulfillment and support. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Authorized staff may access account and gameplay information to provide support, moderate the game, or investigate security issues.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to safety.

Browser storage

The web game uses browser cookies and local storage to keep you signed in. Clearing site data may sign you out. Stripe and other external sites have their own privacy and storage practices.

Retention and deletion

We currently have no scheduled deletion period for inactive accounts, support records, payment records, or backups. We retain these records indefinitely, subject to applicable law and deletion rights.

Self-service account deletion is unavailable. You can email nerfskidevelopment@gmail.com to submit a privacy request, including a request to delete personal information where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Where required or permitted by law, we may retain records needed for accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain uses, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy regulator. Email nerfskidevelopment@gmail.com with your username and the type of request. Please leave your password and full payment details out of your message.

Children’s privacy

Ghoul Valley is intended for players aged 13 and older. Players aged 13–17 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian before purchasing Shards.

A parent or guardian can contact nerfskidevelopment@gmail.com with concerns about a child’s account or information.

Changes and contact

We’ll post updates to this policy here and revise the effective date. For privacy requests, contact Nerfski Development at nerfskidevelopment@gmail.com.
